subject=Improve "invalid symbol" syntax errors
body=When parsing an expression and failing on the very first token, throw
a better message than "invalid symbol":

* "expected statement" in the general case;
* "expected expression" when need an expression;
* "expected condition" after `while`, `if`, `elseif`, `until`;
* "expected identifier or field" when need an lvalue.

subject=Fix .first_token saving for expressions in parens
body=A location for `(a)` or `(a)()` points to `a`, not `(`. Therefore,
.first_token should also be `a`. Get first_token recursively from inner
calls instead of getting it before the first parse_subexpression call
in case the expression starts with a paren.

Fixes incorrect token range for unreachable conditions in parens.
